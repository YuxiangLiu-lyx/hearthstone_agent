# Public response and counterfactual data

The requested `krush_original_deck_training_data.zip` was absent from searched local project/worktree, attachment, Downloads and Desktop paths. Its README/manifest could therefore not be inspected. We do not claim to reproduce its 2,215/1,380 records or 389/480 result. Existing DGM counterfactual files and their source-policy/conditional-state caveats were read instead; hashes and counts are in `data-manifest.json`.

`opponent_replies.jsonl` is newly collected from frozen Y1, seeds 960000–960039, six frozen core opponents and both seats (480 matches). It has 2,962 response windows. Features are captured immediately before our end-turn action; labels come only from subsequent events visible to our seat. Terminal responses are included when visible. The `audit` object contains grouping/diagnostic fields and is never passed to feature generation. Unplayed cards are not labeled absent from hand.

Labels are independent and may co-occur: develop = a public minion play or listed summon spell; buff = one of the explicitly listed public buff/aura cards played with its applicability conditions; taunt = taunt or known taunt-granting public play; removal = non-attack damage to our minion during an opposing action (includes knives, not necessarily a kill); burn = non-attack damage to our hero; visible_trade = opposing attack directed away from our hero. These operational definitions are narrower than a complete semantic card taxonomy and are recorded in `data.py`.

Seed modulo 5 determines fit (2/3/4: 24 seeds, 1,804 rows), calibration (1: 8 seeds, 575 rows), and test (0: 8 seeds, 583 rows). Both seats, every opponent and related states stay together. Labels and test results are used for offline research selection; this is not the final match holdout. `models-offline-only.json` and `bigram-filtered-offline.json` are fitted/source artifacts excluded from runtime packages.

`turn-starts.json` contains public starts of own turns 2–4 and original Y1 plans, with audit metadata. `counterfactual*.jsonl` records course-engine complete-turn alternatives (including shared first actions), public-state gates, selection and validation future RNG streams, and full continuation outcomes. Search labels (public transitions) are separate from terminal win labels. Source hidden hands are retained only inside offline engine copies; these are conditional diagnostics, not public-belief estimates or unknown-hand guarantees. After any public-state divergence, a saved prefix is abandoned and the same Y1 public continuation policy replans.

The first 24-case pilot had a sampling-tool defect: lexicographic strata filled only Astra board/sticky. Its results remain visible. The improved sampler interleaves opponents, and the next 72-case study covers six opponents, both seats, original wins/losses and own turns 2/3/4. This is a concrete harness iteration used for subsequent training. Development win-rate comparisons and prediction tests are separate from final seeds 961000–961079 and 962000–962019.
