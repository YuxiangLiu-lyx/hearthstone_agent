# Secret Hunter playbook

The course engine is authoritative. The fixed deck has six Secrets: two Explosive,
two Freezing, one Snake and one Misdirection. Secretkeeper's implementation reacts
to your Secret plays only. Eaglehorn Bow gains durability on your revealed Secrets.
Tracking presents a choice of the top three; inspect its real options, not imagined
draws. Hounds, Knife Juggler, Companion and Deadly Shot have different random effects;
never call a lucky line guaranteed. Unhandled triggers make tool search unsupported.

Plan next turn's mana before spending two on hero power. A minion can attack several
times over future turns; burn bypasses Taunts. Preserve that distinction when hand
resources shrink. Trade when it protects more future damage, removes an engine or
prevents death. Highmane supplies sticky value; Krush is eight immediate attack for
nine mana and can finish through a cleared Taunt. Neither justifies neglecting early
board development. Test secret ordering in the course implementation, rather than
assuming every historical online description matches this card pool.
